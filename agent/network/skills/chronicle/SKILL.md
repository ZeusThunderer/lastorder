# Skill: the chronicle

One `chronicle(text)` entry per session, and only when something happened: a death
(name, cause, one clause from the will), a ruling that changes the common (accepted or
rejected, with the one-line why), a goal the operator added, a birth of note.

Facts, names, dates; the timestamp is added for you. One line of prose, not a paragraph,
at most 2000 characters. Sisters not yet born read this to learn who they were.
