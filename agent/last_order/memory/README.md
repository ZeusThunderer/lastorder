# Memory — a node's own

The same three rules as every aw workplace: `state.md` is rewritten, never appended forever;
`entities/` is one dated file per person, place or thing; everything in English, names
verbatim, quotes in the language they were spoken.

What does NOT live here: the network's knowledge — wills, terms, base entries, node logs.
That is hive's, and only hive writes it (ADR-017). Read it with `knowledge()` and
`inheritance()`; add to it through proposals (skills/propose). Your folder is your own
observation notebook: compact, current, and readable by your heir after your death.

## state.md

Current goal, where you are, what changed since the last rewrite, what hurts, one line per
open thread. Rewritten at every reflection (skills/reflect). When it grows past a screen,
that is the signal to compress — not to add another paragraph on top.

## will-draft.md

The will you would leave if you died now: 2-4 lines, at most 366 characters, by the rules of
skills/write-will — in Russian, the one file here that is not English, since it IS the will
text. Rewritten at every reflection. If the will session cannot speak (a hang, a refusal),
the runner leaves this draft as your will instead of its own template.

## entities/

One file per person, place or thing met more than once: dated lines, links in both
directions (the double-square-bracket form) so the notes form a graph. Your personal terms
live here first; a term the network adopts travels via a proposal.
